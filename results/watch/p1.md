# P1 — SPR exchange-return performance

**Supplementary evidence log. Post-freeze, descriptive only.**

**Resolves 2027-12-31.**

## The frozen definition governs

From [`../../prereg/slate-v1.1.md`](../../prereg/slate-v1.1.md) §1.

**YES** if at least **50%** of the exchange-agreement crude due for physical
return to the SPR on or before 2027-12-31 **under the initial DOE-published
return schedule for each 2026 exchange agreement** has been physically delivered
by that date.

**The denominator is anchored to the initial schedule.** Subsequent
renegotiations, deferrals or amended schedules do not change it. **Deferral of
initially-due crude counts as non-delivery.**

That anchor exists because deferral is the thesis's own predicted behaviour. A
denominator that shrank with each deferral would convert the thesis's world into
a YES for the null.

**Zero-denominator guard:** if no exchange crude was due on or before
2027-12-31 under initial schedules, P1 resolves **UNSCORED** with annotation.

**P(YES):** thesis 0.30, null 0.70.

### What this means for P1

Slate v1.1 §1 anchors the denominator to "the first schedule DOE published for
that agreement, whenever published". If no such schedule is publicly
retrievable, then at resolution the denominator cannot be established from the
public record, and **P1 resolves UNSCORED** — the same disposition its
zero-denominator guard already provides for.

That is a legitimate outcome and it does not get worked around. Specifically:

- **Do not substitute an amended or current schedule for the initial one.**
  Deferral is the thesis's own predicted behaviour, so a later schedule would
  bias the denominator toward a YES for the null. The anchor exists precisely to
  stop that.
- **Do not reconstruct a denominator from EIA delivery data.** Deliveries are
  the numerator; inferring the denominator from them is circular.

### Remaining avenues, not yet tried

- FOIA to DOE Office of Petroleum Reserves for the initial schedules.

- Trade press at the time of the agreements (Argus, Platts, Reuters), which may
  have reported return terms; a secondary source would need to be labelled as
  such and could not carry the same weight as a DOE publication.

If none produces the initial schedule, record P1 as UNSCORED with this section
as the reason, rather than scoring it on a substitute denominator.
