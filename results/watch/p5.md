## The frozen definition governs

From [`../../prereg/slate-v1.1.md`](../../prereg/slate-v1.1.md) §5. Reproduced
here so entries are logged against it, not against impressions.

**YES (pause holds):** no resumed exchange from 2026-07-28 through 2026-12-31.

**Resumed exchange** requires, within any rolling **7-calendar-day** window
(days in Arabian Standard Time, UTC+3):

- **(a)** one side conducts kinetic strikes against the qualifying target set on
  **three or more distinct calendar days**, AND
- **(b)** the other side conducts **at least one** qualifying kinetic strike
  within the same window.

Symmetric — either side may be the ≥3-day striker, and no causal ordering is
implied.

**Does not qualify:** proxy action without attributed Iranian direction (Houthi
strikes alone), covert sabotage, cyber operations, single strikes, or
sub-threshold patterns.

**Scored on behavior, not rhetoric.** Refusing the word "ceasefire" while holding
fire is a hold. Diplomatic labels, threats and posturing are non-events.

**P(YES):** thesis 0.40, null 0.75.

## Assessment notes

- **The lateral incidents are the ones to watch carefully.** Each has to be
  tested against attribution: an incident without Iranian direction attributed
  by two first-tier outlets or an official US/Israeli statement does not
  qualify, however suggestive.
- **The rolling-window structure means a run of strike days matters more than
  any single event.** The v1.0 definition failed its own backtest by requiring
  consecutive same-day reciprocal strikes; v1.1 was repaired to catch the July
  2026 campaign. Log strike *days* so the window can be evaluated.
- **Nothing here resolves early.** A NO is only reached when the definition is
  met inside a 7-day window. Accumulating tension is not a trigger.
