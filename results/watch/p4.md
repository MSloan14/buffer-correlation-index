# P4 — fourth consecutive annual overdose decline

**Supplementary evidence log. Post-freeze, descriptive only.**

**Resolves ~2027-06-30**, backstop 2027-09-30.

## The frozen definition governs

From [`../../prereg/slate-v1.1.md`](../../prereg/slate-v1.1.md) §4.

**YES** if NCHS-reported total US drug-overdose deaths for calendar 2026 are
lower than for calendar 2025, **both figures as published in the same release** —
the first NCHS annual provisional release covering CY2026.

Same-release comparison is required to neutralise the provisional-revision
channel. Comparing across releases would let revisions decide the outcome.

**Backstop:** if that release has not appeared by 2027-09-30, resolve on the CDC
provisional 12-month-ending-December-2026 estimate as published at that date,
against the 12-month-ending-December-2025 estimate **in the same publication**.

**Policy-confound rule.** P4 tests the strong "bodies register the macro" claim
only. If declines reverse following documented cuts to Medicaid or prevention
funding, the reversal **scores for the null** with annotation — it is evidence
about public-health-buffer withdrawal, not about macro-despair. A NO is
adjudicated for attribution before scoring.

**P(YES):** thesis 0.35, null 0.70.

**This is the slate's anti-thesis entry.** The thesis-as-written is embarrassed
by continued decline. It is in the slate because a framework has to be scoreable
where it is weakest.

## Attribution watch

Because a NO must be adjudicated for attribution before it scores, documented
funding changes need logging **as they happen**, not reconstructed afterwards:

- Medicaid coverage or eligibility changes
- Prevention, naloxone distribution or treatment funding changes
- Supply-side shifts (fentanyl adulteration, supply disruption) that the null's
  account would predict independently of macro conditions

An entry here does not resolve anything. It preserves the record needed to apply
the confound rule honestly at resolution, rather than arguing attribution after
seeing the number.
