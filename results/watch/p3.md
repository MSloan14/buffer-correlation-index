> **T = M₂₀₂₅ + 0.25 percentage points**, where M₂₀₂₅ is the mean of the twelve
> monthly ACM 10-year term-premium values for calendar 2025, computed from the
> NY Fed series vintage available **on the resolution date**.

Reference value only: T ≈ 0.75%. The formula governs, not the reference.

**YES** if the mean of the twelve monthly ACM 10-year term-premium values for
calendar 2027, from that same resolution-date vintage, exceeds T.

**Both means come from one vintage.** The ACM model is retrospectively
re-estimated and published history shifts across vintages, so mixing vintages
would let the revision decide the outcome.

**P(YES):** thesis 0.65, null 0.30.
