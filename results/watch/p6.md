# P6 — the index's own out-of-sample trajectory

**Supplementary evidence log. Post-freeze, descriptive only.**

**Resolves 2029-03-31.** Scaffold only — no entries yet.

## The frozen definition governs

From [`../../prereg/slate-v1.1.md`](../../prereg/slate-v1.1.md) §6.

**Instrument:** the quarterly companion of Index Spec v0.2 §7 (S8) — mean
pairwise Pearson correlation of first-differenced normalized series over the
trailing 40-quarter window, on the quarterly-native subset: strategic reserve,
financial, fiscal debt/GDP, household. Six pairs.

**Outcome:** the companion statistic at **2028Q4 minus its value at 2026Q4**,
computable around 2029-03-31.

**Bands:** RISE above +0.05 · FLAT within ±0.05 · DECLINE below −0.05.

**P(outcome):** thesis — RISE 0.55, FLAT 0.30, DECLINE 0.15. Null — RISE 0.25,
FLAT 0.40, DECLINE 0.35.

## P6 stands, and the instrument does not move

The index test was withdrawn ([`../withdrawal-note.md`](../withdrawal-note.md)),
and **P6 was not withdrawn with it.** It is scored at resolution exactly as
defined above.

A prediction registered in public does not get retired because the project
around it changed shape, and the measuring instrument does not get redefined
because a later finding made it inconvenient. **The S8 companion as specified in
v0.2 is the instrument, whatever any later specification says.**

## The caveat, disclosed now rather than at resolution

The curvature confound that withdrew the index applies to this statistic too. It
is a differencing-based measure, so a shared depletion path that bends is read as
rising co-movement.

**Therefore a RISE outcome will be consistent with two different worlds** — genuinely
rising co-movement, and a shared acceleration in depletion rates — and **P6
cannot distinguish them.**

This is recorded in 2026, before any resolution, so that it cannot later be
raised by whichever side it happens to suit. A RISE does not vindicate the
withdrawn claim. A DECLINE is not evidence against it. The prediction is scored
because it was made, and its evidential value is limited by this in a way that
was known in advance.
