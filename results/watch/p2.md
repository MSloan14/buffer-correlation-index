# P2 — systemic private-credit event

**Supplementary evidence log. Post-freeze, descriptive only.**

**Resolves 2029-12-31.**

## The frozen definition governs

From [`../../prereg/slate-v1.1.md`](../../prereg/slate-v1.1.md) §2.

**YES** if, on or before 2029-12-31, at least one of:

- **(i)** an official-sector emergency action aimed at the private-credit / BDC /
  interval-fund complex — a Federal Reserve facility, a Treasury or FSOC
  emergency measure, or an explicit regulatory forbearance program responding to
  private-credit distress;
- **(ii)** within any rolling 90-day window, suspension or gating of
  redemptions, or payment default on fund-level obligations, at **≥3 unaffiliated
  private-credit vehicles with combined AUM ≥ $50B**;
- **(iii)** failure or forced rescue of a **top-10-by-private-credit-AUM**
  manager.

**Explicitly NOT sufficient:** a single fund failing, NAV markdowns however deep,
spread widening, or concentrated losses at LPs without contagion. That is the
"concentrated losses" world and it **scores for the null**.

**P(YES):** thesis 0.60, null 0.20.

## Why neither entry counts, stated plainly

Both are logged because they are relevant context for the mechanism. **Neither
moves P2 at all**, and the log should not be read as though they do.

The definition was written to exclude exactly this kind of evidence. Large
off-balance-sheet obligations and a single downgrade are the "concentrated
losses" world, which the frozen text says **scores for the null**. Recording them
as though they were partial progress toward YES would be scoring against the
definition instead of with it.

## Mechanics worth tracking (not evidence)

Two structural notes that determine *when* obligations would become visible.
These are reasons to keep watching, not events:

- Leases roll **on**-balance-sheet at facility go-live, so reported figures rise
  mechanically as capacity comes online, without anything new going wrong.
- Impairments land on **lenders**, not on the tenant, so distress would surface
  first in the credit vehicles holding the exposure — which is where criteria
  (ii) and (iii) look.

## What would actually count

- A Fed facility, Treasury/FSOC measure, or explicit forbearance program aimed at
  this complex.
- Three or more unaffiliated vehicles gating or defaulting inside 90 days, with
  combined AUM ≥ $50B. **Count vehicles and AUM, not headlines.**
- A top-10 manager failing or being rescued.

Anything short of that is context.
